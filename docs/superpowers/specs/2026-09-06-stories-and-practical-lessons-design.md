# Instagram companion Stories and practical lessons

Every newly planned campaign and still-unpublished existing plan opts into one Instagram Story. Publish its existing
vertical video after Instagram confirms the primary post. Keep a separate Story
record, commit intent before the provider write, reconcile ambiguous outcomes,
and never automatically repeat an uncertain create. Existing campaign copy and
media remain unchanged. Already-published historical posts are not backfilled.
Instagram disablement applies to both placements.

The scheduled workflow processes Stories after reconciliation. This means delivery
follows confirmation on the next workflow run (currently every three hours), not
an instantaneous webhook. Controlled runs do not create Stories; confirmed posts
are picked up by the next scheduled run.
Dry runs never create provider objects. Failed Stories surface in health checks.

Add twelve sourced lessons: four scam-awareness, four mental-math, and four
workplace habits. New recipes start on 2026-09-07 and preserve old stored plans.
Each lesson has a hook, situation, takeaway, and closing action, using the approved
centered 9:16 design and soundtrack pool. Avoid unrelated change calculations in
lesson videos and captions. Do not promise promotions or guaranteed protection.
